About Me

Who I Am

I am a dedicated trainer passionate about empowering individuals through digital skills, social innovation, and martial arts. I specialize in equipping young people, particularly vulnerable girls and women, with essential digital literacy and self-defense skills.

My goal is to create a positive impact in communities by providing accessible education and training that builds confidence, technical abilities, and personal safety awareness.

With experience in software development, digital facilitation, and martial arts coaching, I bring a unique blend of technical knowledge and physical discipline to all my training programs.
